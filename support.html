Support

Last updated November 7, 2026

Need help with Skyframe, found a bug, or want to report a name on the World board? Write to skyframe1992@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without one. Saving a score to the World board makes a guest account for your device; on iPhone and iPad you can sign in with Apple, and on Android with Google, to keep your entry when you change phones.

How do I change my name or flag?

In the main menu, tap the person button, edit the name or tap the flag, then Save.

How do I report or hide a player?

On the World board, tap ⋯ next to their name.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account.
