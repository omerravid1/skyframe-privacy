Names on the World board

Names you choose are shown publicly next to your score and flag. A name must not:

  • be offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or harassing;
  • target a person or group because of who they are;
  • include personal information such as a full name, phone number or address;
  • impersonate another person, brand or Skyframe itself;
  • advertise anything or include links.

The game filters common offensive words. Any player can report a name or hide a player from their own view of the board.

Moderation

We review reported names within 24 hours and may, without notice, remove a name or an entry, or block an account from the board, if it breaks these rules or if a score looks impossible. If we remove something of yours you can write to us to ask why and to contest it.
