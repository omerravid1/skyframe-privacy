Delete your account

Last updated November 7, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Skyframe account and the data that goes with it. It applies to the game on iPhone and iPad (App Store), Android (Google Play) and the web.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Skyframe and go to the main menu.
  2. Tap the person button, next to the sound button.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm. If you signed in with Apple or Google, you are asked to sign in once more to confirm it's you.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the game

Email skyframe1992@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Skyframe account" and include your player ID (shown in the game under the person button) or, if you no longer have the game, your board name, flag, score and roughly when you saved it. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your online account (guest, Apple or Google sign-in), and for Apple, the game's access to your Apple ID is revoked;
  • your entry on the World board: name, flag, score and its details;
  • any reports filed about your name.

What is kept

Nothing on our side. Copies in our provider's (Google Firebase) backups are erased on its normal schedule, within 180 days. Progress saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear the site's data.
