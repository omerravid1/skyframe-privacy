Skyframe — Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Skyframe is a falling-block puzzle game. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Skyframe has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send any personal or usage data to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (such as the stages you have unlocked) and settings (such as language and sound on or off) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Skyframe does not ask for any device permissions such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

Skyframe does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
