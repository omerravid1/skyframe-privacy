Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Skyframe is a free falling-block puzzle game. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells data. This policy explains the little data the game does handle, and your rights over it.

Who is responsible

Skyframe, an independent game, is the controller of the data described here. Contact: skyframe1992@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Your progress (stages unlocked, best scores, My scores), settings (language, look, sound) and the name and flag you last used are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you uninstall the game or clear the site's data.

Data used for the World high-score board

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to save a score to the World board, sign in, or report a name. Then:

DataWhyWho can see it
A player ID: a random ID that Firebase gives your device (a "guest" account) the first time you save a scoreTo know which board entry is yoursOnly us and our provider
The name you type (3–12 characters) and the flag you choose (a country code, or none)To show your entry on the boardEveryone who opens the board

The flag starts as the region set on your device (for example, Israel for "Hebrew (Israel)"). The game never uses your location.

Legal basis

We process this data to provide the board and the account you asked for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the board safe and free of abuse (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes the data

The board and accounts run on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google as our processor. Board data is stored in the European Union (Firestore region "eur3"); Firebase Authentication may process data in the United States. Transfers outside the EU rely on Google's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in Google's data processing terms. Apple and Google handle sign-in under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Your board entry and account stay until you delete them. Reports are deleted once reviewed, within 90 days. After you delete your account, copies in our provider's backups are erased on its normal schedule (Google states up to 180 days).

Your rights

You can delete your account and board entry at any time in the game: open the menu, tap the person button, then Delete my account. You can also ask us by email (see Delete your account). Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to receive it in a portable form; write to skyframe1992@gmail.com and we answer within one month. You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Children

Skyframe is meant for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has saved a score, write to us and we will delete it.

Cookies and browser storage

The web version stores your progress and, if you save a score, your Firebase session in your browser's storage. This is strictly necessary for the features you use; there are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
